Built for agents, too

Give your AI agent a phone.

My Side Call is also a connector (MCP server). Any AI agent — Claude, Muse, your own — can place a real call, get the transcript, answer a mid-call question, or call its owner for a wake-up or check-in. Same safety rules apply.

// MCP tools exposed by My Side Call
place_call({
  to: "+1 201 555 0142",
  goal: "Move my dental cleaning to next week, afternoons only",
  owner_language: "ko",
  ask_owner_before_agreeing: true
})
get_call_status({ call_id })        // live transcript, pending question
answer_question({ call_id, answer }) // your reply goes into the call
call_owner({ message: "Your 9am meeting moved to 10." }) // the agent phones you
Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Does it make real phone calls?

Yes. My Side Call dials real U.S. phone numbers and holds a real conversation. You watch the transcript live and can end the call at any time.

What is the AI behind the call?

The conversation, the mid-call questions and the summary are run by Meta's Muse Spark model through the Meta Model API. Speech-to-text and the phone line come from Vapi.

Will the AI agree to something without asking me?

No. Anything that wasn't in your request — a new time, a fee, a change of plan — is sent to your screen as a question in your language. The AI keeps the line open until you answer.

Can other AI agents use My Side Call?

Yes, through the MCP connector. An agent can place calls on your behalf and can also call you — for example a morning wake-up call that reads your schedule.

What won't it do?

Emergency calls (911), medical or legal judgment calls, and anything that requires impersonating you in a legally binding way. Calls are recorded for your summary; the other party is told they are speaking with an assistant when asked.
