What My Side Call does

You write what you need
In Korean, Spanish, Japanese, Chinese or English. Snap a photo of a bill and it reads the account number and amount for you.
The AI makes a real phone call
In natural English, to any US number. It says it is an AI assistant in its first sentence and that the call may be recorded.
It asks you before deciding
A new time, an extra fee — anything not in your request comes to your screen as a question in your language. The AI keeps the line open until you answer.
You get a summary in your language
What was agreed, what is still open, what to put on your calendar — plus the recording and a clean transcript.
Your other AI can use it too
Muse, Claude or any agent can connect and place calls for you, or call you — a wake-up call, a reminder, a result.
Built with Meta
Muse Spark runs the conversation, the summary and the bill reading. Muse Voice Transcribe re-transcribes the recording. Facebook Login signs you in.
What it will not do
Emergency calls (911), medical or legal judgment calls, or agreeing to anything you did not approve.
Where this goes
Today: your language to English, in the US. Next: English to any language, in any country. Later: two people, two languages, one live call.
